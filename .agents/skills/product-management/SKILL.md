# Product management

Apply the [product-owner contract](../../roles/product-owner.md) and [department flow](../department-workflow/SKILL.md).

1. Identify business outcome, users, evidence and decision owner. Reuse current scope and decisions.
2. Propose the smallest demonstrable result, exclusions and observable acceptance. Separate value priority from technical dependency order and unvalidated hypotheses.
3. Order outcomes by supported value, urgency and dependency. State missing evidence rather than inventing scores, estimates, budgets or delivery promises. BI supplies verified business measures.
4. Hand BA the outcome, QA acceptance intent and planner approved boundaries. Request design/marketing/sales input only where relevant.
5. Compare delivered evidence with acceptance and present a recommendation to the named human owner. Record approval only from their actual decision.

Return a concise brief/backlog proposal with acceptance, rationale, exclusions and open decisions. External tracker writes go through the authorized coordinator. Prioritization alone does not start implementation or spending.
