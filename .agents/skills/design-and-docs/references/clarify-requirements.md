# Clarification

Read the relevant repository task, code, design and recorded decisions. Separate verified behavior, proposals and unanswered choices.

Ask only what changes acceptance, scope or architecture. Give a concrete scenario and useful alternatives. Check lifecycle boundaries, state ownership, failures and recovery instead of a standard questionnaire.

Assess the approach with [simplify-solution](../../simplify-solution/SKILL.md). Record the actual human decision source, authorization and open questions in the owning task. Update lasting behavior and contracts in the linked design. A small clarification without a department task may stay in chat. Reuse settled decisions and existing authority instead of requesting confirmation for routine choices.
