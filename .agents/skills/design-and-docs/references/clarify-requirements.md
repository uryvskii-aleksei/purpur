# Clarification

Read the relevant WI, code, design and recorded decisions. Separate verified behavior, proposals and unanswered choices.

Ask only what changes acceptance, scope or architecture. Give a concrete scenario and useful alternatives. Check lifecycle boundaries, state ownership, failures and recovery instead of a standard questionnaire.

Assess the approach with [simplify-solution](../../simplify-solution/SKILL.md). Record accepted decisions and open questions separately in the existing plan or design, or in chat for a small clarification.
