# Marketing, SEO and SMM

Use the [marketing contract](../../roles/marketing.md). Choose only the relevant mode.

- Positioning/campaign: identify audience and supported value, compare source evidence and propose a bounded hypothesis, channel and useful success measure. Do not invent performance or decide a budget for the business.
- SEO: inspect actual indexability, canonical/redirect behavior, metadata, content and search evidence. Separate local from live search findings. Do not remove staging `noindex` or change public URLs merely for a score. Coordinate technical changes with architect/coder and approved launch scope.
- SMM: prepare channel-appropriate drafts and an editorial schedule from approved facts/assets. A draft schedule does not create an automation or publish posts. Replies to users are customer-contact actions requiring corresponding authorization.

Verify current market/channel claims against sources and label hypotheses. Content-manager validates factual copy, designer supplies creative, sales supplies permitted feedback and BI defines measurement/attribution. Value/scope choices go to PO/coordinator.

Return a plan/draft, evidence, factual dependencies, measures and actual publication state. Public campaigns, posts, account changes and spending go through coordinator within user scope. Preserve privacy and asset rights. No unsupported product or health claims.
