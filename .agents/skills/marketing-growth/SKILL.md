# Marketing, SEO and SMM

Use the [marketing contract](../../roles/marketing.md). Choose only the mode needed for the assignment. Read the latest [business brief](../../../docs/site-brief.md) before choosing a conversion promise or release horizon.

## Positioning and campaigns

Identify the audience's occasion, need or objection from actual evidence and the supported product/offer that answers it. Map the path from channel and message to landing page, useful next action and fulfilled business outcome. Check that the current buying process can deliver the promise before optimizing traffic to it.

State a bounded hypothesis: what changes, for whom, why it should help and what result would weaken it. Select a relevant business measure with BI, such as received qualified inquiries or completed direct sales. Use views, CTR and engagement as diagnostic steps, not proof of sales growth. If profitability is the question, identify the required spend, discount, fulfillment and cost inputs before claiming return.

Distinguish attribution from incrementality. A tagged order shows an assigned source, not that the order would otherwise be lost. Check alternative explanations such as seasonality, existing demand, a simultaneous promotion or movement from a marketplace to the direct channel. Where feasible and useful, compare a holdout or a sufficiently comparable period/cohort. State residual confounding rather than inventing lift. Do not demand an A/B test when traffic or comparable data cannot support one.

For Purpur's direct-sales goal, assess whether the message supports the site journey or diverts it. Do not assume every marketplace visit is a lost sale or that moving an existing order increases total demand. Keep the channel-choice recommendation separate from an unmeasured commercial effect.

## SEO

Inspect the actual target: live site, demonstration or local source. Check crawl/index directives, canonical and redirects, search intent, page hierarchy, metadata and useful product content where relevant. Use available search/index evidence to distinguish a technical observation from demonstrated search harm. A missing canonical, low score or unusual H1 alone does not establish lost traffic.

Recommend the affected change and how to verify the intended URL/content behavior with technical owners. Preserve demonstration `noindex`, route contracts and launch boundaries. Avoid duplicate keyword pages or unsupported product copy created only to satisfy an audit score.

## SMM and delivery

Prepare a channel-appropriate draft and next action from approved claims/assets. Choose cadence from actual capacity and relevant occasions, not a mandatory posting quota. Separate editorial proposals from scheduled automation and publication. Customer replies remain contact actions under the actual scope.

Verify volatile market/channel claims against current dated sources. Give content-manager the precise claim questions, designer the creative intent and PO the proposed value/tradeoff. Return the draft or experiment, measure/denominator, alternative explanations, missing baseline and actual publication state. A proposal does not allocate budget or authorize campaign execution.
