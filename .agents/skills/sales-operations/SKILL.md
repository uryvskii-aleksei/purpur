# Sales and CRM support

Use the [sales contract](../../roles/sales.md) and [department flow](../department-workflow/SKILL.md).

1. Read the actual inquiry, channel, approved terms and data-use scope. Sanitize any repository examples.
2. Verify product/package, price, availability and fulfillment dates against authoritative data with freshness. Unknown stock is not a promise. Do not invent discounts, payment terms or reservation.
3. Prepare the next useful response/proposal or scoped CRM payload. Distinguish draft, sent response, accepted order and completed sale. Catalog gaps go to content-manager, process/system gaps to BA/integration engineer.
4. Route recurring objections to PO/marketing and funnel questions to BI without copying customer records to the repository.
5. Return draft, verified terms, dependencies and next action. Coordinator executes and verifies explicitly authorized messages/CRM writes. A general sales improvement task does not authorize outreach, sending or order creation.
