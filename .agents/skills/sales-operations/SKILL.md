# Sales and CRM support

Use the [sales contract](../../roles/sales.md). Consult the current [business brief](../../../docs/site-brief.md) for the boundary between manager-led payment now and the future online store.

1. Read the actual question and any permitted prior conversation before asking for data again. Identify the intended result: retail purchase, store supply, corporate gifts or an unresolved interest. A CRM widget or a contact address does not establish a managed sales process.
2. Qualify for the next action, not for a complete customer profile. For a first useful answer, a contact and stated need may be enough. For calculation, determine the material missing detail: exact product/package, quantity/unit, desired date, destination or budget where relevant. Answer supported parts now. Do not demand company requisites, every delivery address or a fixed budget before they are needed.
3. Separate buyer preferences from approved commitments. Verify package, price basis, freshness, availability and fulfillment feasibility against their actual owners/sources. A desired date is not a delivery promise, unknown stock is not a reserve and a large quantity does not prove a discount entitlement. Record requests for branding or unusual fulfillment as requests until confirmed.
4. Prepare the useful reply or quote. Make clear what is included, the quantity/unit, goods amount, separately known charges and the status of payment/fulfillment terms. Use a validity period, MOQ or discount only if approved. If a calculation is blocked, explain the specific dependency and the next useful question rather than inventing the missing amount.
5. For a revised quote, identify the current version and the changed products, quantities or terms. Retain the distinction between calculated draft, actually sent offer, buyer agreement, confirmed order and confirmed payment. A prepared reply or a customer's silence cannot advance those states. Route receipt/ownership/recovery gaps to BA/integration engineer instead of designing an assumed CRM workflow.
6. Return a ready-to-use draft or scoped change payload with the next actor/action and unresolved material terms. Route recurring objections to PO/marketing and funnel definitions to BI. Keep customer records in their approved system and use sanitized examples in repository artifacts. Coordinator executes authorized contact/CRM changes and verifies actual delivery, not merely preparation.

Do not replace the current manager-led payment process with checkout integration as part of a reply or workflow-improvement task. Future online payment, customer identity and self-service remain separate scope.
